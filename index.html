Lesson Plan: Upgrading Vocabulary for IELTS Speaking

Level: Intermediate / Upper-Intermediate | Focus: IELTS Speaking Part 1 & Part 2

Primary Goal: Replace simple words and Thai-transcription habits with precise collocations, idiomatic phrases, and descriptive adjectives.
Phase 1
10 Minutes

Warm-up & Diagnostic

Light conversation to assess natural vocabulary range before introducing new material. Do not correct immediately—jot down overused basic words.

Q1

"Tell me about your hometown or where you live now."

Q2

"What do you like to do in your free time?"

Q3

"How do you usually like to relax after a busy day?"

Diagnostic Target: Watch for repetitive basic words: nice, fun, relaxed, busy, good food. Note them down in the scratchpad below.
Phase 2
15 Minutes

The "Upgrade" Strategy

Replace general words or literal Thai-to-English translations with Band 7.5+ native collocations across 3 core topics.

Topic 1

Talking About Places & Food

Basic / Overused Phrase Band 7.5+ Upgrade
"The city is very crowded and busy."
"It's a bustling metropolis with a vibrant nightlife."
"The food is very delicious."
"It's mouth-watering / full of flavor."
"It's quiet and peaceful."
"It's a serene place, perfect to escape the hustle and bustle."
Topic 2

Talking About Free Time & Hobbies

"I really like listening to music."
"I'm a big fan of listening to music; it helps me unwind."
"It makes me feel happy/relaxed."
"It's a great way to recharge my batteries or decompress."
"I do it when I have time."
"I try to fit it in once in a blue moon / whenever I have a spare moment."
Topic 3

Talking About Work, Study, or Life

"I have a lot of work / hard work."
"I have a heavy workload / it's quite demanding."
"It's very difficult."
"It can be quite challenging / a steep learning curve."
Phase 3
15 Minutes

Controlled Practice & Substitution

Re-speak and re-write basic sentences using target vocabulary. Immediate positive reinforcement & prompt hints.

"My hometown is famous for good food and quiet parks."
Target: mouth-watering serene escape the hustle and bustle
Upgraded Response: "My hometown is renowned for its mouth-watering cuisine and serene parks, providing the perfect spot to escape the hustle and bustle."
"I go shopping when I am free because it makes me happy."
Target: spare moment unwind recharge my batteries
Upgraded Response: "Whenever I have a spare moment, I love going shopping to unwind and recharge my batteries."
"Studying English is hard, but I try every day."
Target: challenging demanding steep learning curve
Upgraded Response: "Studying English can be quite demanding with a steep learning curve, but I remain dedicated every single day."
Phase 4
15 Minutes

  • Where it is

Grammar & Collocation

Cuisine & Geography (Proximity)

Grammar Correction & Usage:

"The local cuisine comprises a variety of herbs."

Grammar Key: Comprise means "to consist of" or "be made up of".
• Active: "The cuisine comprises a variety of herbs." (No "of" needed!)
• Passive: "The cuisine is comprised of a variety of herbs."
Bangkok Proximity & Pricing:
  • "It has a major influence from nearby Bangkok."
  • "As my town is in close proximity to Bangkok, it is very pricey."
Formulating Questions with "Proximity":

"What's the proximity of the cafe to the train station?"

Noun | /prɒkˈsɪm.ə.ti/ — state of being near in space or time
Vocabulary Nuance

Metropolitan vs. Cosmopolitan

Understanding city adjectives for high IELTS Lexical Resource scores:

Metropolitan

Focuses on urban size, infrastructure, and big-city density.

e.g. "a sprawling metropolitan area"

Cosmopolitan

Focuses on cultural diversity, international flair, and global vibe.

e.g. "Tokyo, Bangkok, London, New York are vibrant cosmopolitan cities."
"Cocktail Cosmopolitan" vs "Cosmopolitan City":

Don't confuse the famous cocktail with the descriptive adjective for diverse world capitals!

Pronunciation & Form

Verb Focus: Wind & Unwind

Present Simple: Wind / Unwind /waɪnd/ & /ʌnˈwaɪnd/

⚠️ Rhymes with mind, find, kind (NOT wind/air).

Past Simple / Participle: Wound / Unwound /waʊnd/ & /ʌnˈwaʊnd/

⚠️ Rhymes with round, sound, found.

Collocation Meaning: To relax, decrease tension, or recover energy after hard work.

"Listening to classical music helps me unwind after a demanding day."
