# IELTS Speaking Masterclass: Vocabulary Upgrade Hub

Welcome to the **IELTS Speaking Masterclass Learning Hub**, specifically designed for Intermediate and Upper-Intermediate students aiming to elevate their Lexical Resource score to **Band 7.5+**.

Live Web App: [https://log210788.github.io/aistudent/](https://log210788.github.io/aistudent/)

---

## 🎯 Lesson Plan Overview

### **Level:** Intermediate / Upper-Intermediate
### **Focus:** IELTS Speaking Part 1 & Part 2 (Lexical Resource)
### **Goal:** Replace basic words and Thai-transcription habits with precise collocations, idiomatic phrases, and descriptive adjectives.

### **Structure (5-Phase Curriculum):**
1. **Phase 1: Warm-up & Diagnostic (10 Mins)**
   - Unscripted initial conversation (Hometown, Free time, Relaxation).
   - Teacher diagnostic observation without interrupting.
2. **Phase 2: The "Upgrade" Strategy (15 Mins)**
   - Systematic replacement of basic words across 3 core topics (*Places & Food*, *Free Time & Hobbies*, *Work/Study/Life*).
3. **Phase 3: Controlled Practice & Substitution (15 Mins)**
   - Guided re-speaking and sentence re-writing drills with target vocabulary.
4. **Phase 4: Mock IELTS Part 2 Challenge (15 Mins)**
   - Cue Card simulation: *Describe a place you like to visit in your free time*.
   - 1-minute prep timer + 2-minute speech delivery requiring at least 3 target upgrades.
5. **Phase 5: Feedback & Wrap-up (5 Mins)**
   - Praising accurate usage, gentle pronunciation refinements (*unwind* vs *wound*), and take-home task assignment.

## 📝 Today's Tidied Live Class Notes

- **Grammar & Collocation:**
  - *Comprise*: *"The local cuisine comprises a variety of herbs."* (Active: no 'of'; Passive: *is comprised of*).
- **Proximity & Geography:**
  - *"It has a major influence from nearby Bangkok."*
  - *"As my town is in close proximity to Bangkok, it is very pricey."*
  - Question phrasing: *"What's the proximity of the cafe to the train station?"*
- **Vocabulary Nuance (City Descriptors):**
  - *Metropolitan*: Infrastructure, size, density.
  - *Cosmopolitan*: Cultural diversity, international vibe (Tokyo, Bangkok, London, New York).
- **Pronunciation Alert (Verb Conjugation):**
  - *Wind / Unwind* (/waɪnd/ & /ʌnˈwaɪnd/): Rhymes with *mind* (to relax after tension).
  - *Wound / Unwound* (/waʊnd/ & /ʌnˈwaʊnd/): Rhymes with *sound* (past tense).
- **Target Vocabulary Upgrades:**
  - Hometown: `mouth-watering`, `serene`, `escape the hustle and bustle`.
  - Free Time: `spare moment`, `unwind`, `recharge my batteries`, `decompress`.
